## 🎯 About the Platform

This platform empowers students in **HSC 27**, **HSC 28**, and **University Admission (BUET / Engineering, Medical & Varsity 'A' Unit)** across Bangladesh to overcome chemistry phobia through deep, visual, first-principles understanding rather than mechanical rote memorization.

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

### 1. ⚛️ Interactive 3D Molecular Physics Engine
- Real-time interactive 3D rotation and orbital rendering for key molecules (**Benzene $C_6H_6$**, **Water $H_2O$**, **Methane $CH_4$**, **Ethanol $C_2H_5OH$**).
- Dynamic scientific telemetry detailing molecular geometry, bond angles, and hybridization states ($sp, sp^2, sp^3$).

### 2. ⚗️ Continuous Chemical Distillation Pipeline Simulation
- An authentic, animated 2D vector chemical laboratory apparatus flow.
- Models gravity separation in a separatory funnel, thermal activation over a glowing Bunsen burner, realistic condensation inside a Liebig water jacket with continuous color transitions, and vacuum-assisted collection in the receiving flask.
- 100% responsive and optimized for mobile screens without horizontal scrollbars.

### 3. 🧠 The Karigor Method™ (কারিগর মেথড)
- 5-step structured conceptual framework:  
  `01 Understand (অনুধাবন)` ➔ `02 Visualize (ভিজুয়ালাইজ)` ➔ `03 Connect (সংযোগ)` ➔ `04 Practice (অনুশীলন)` ➔ `05 Master (পারদর্শিতা)`
- Side-by-side comparison illustrating traditional memorization vs. conceptual mastery.

### 4. 📚 Free Chemistry Resource Library
- Searchable and categorized PDF concept sheets, Organic reaction roadmaps, past Board & Admission question banks, and weekly problem sets.
- Instant modal previews and direct download triggers.

### 5. ❓ Comprehensive FAQ with Real-Time Search
- High-frequency questions covering course curriculum, batch schedules, offline/online classroom setups, exam systems, and tuition fees.
- Real-time search with interactive topic chips.

### 6. 📱 Responsive & Mobile-First
- Handcrafted layout ensuring seamless readability across all devices — from 360px smartphones to 4K displays.
